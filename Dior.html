Inspired by the House's archives as well as Christian Dior's life, Maria Grazia Chiuri imagines sophisticated women's luxury designer bags that deliver a meaningful message. Driven by a powerful feminist vision that drives her fashion shows, the creative director creates women's fashion bags that are both functional and timeless. Iconic luxury bags - like the Lady Dior, Saddle, 30 Montaigne, Dior Book Tote or Dior Bobby – are available in a multitude of motifs, formats and colors that meet the needs of modern women. Women's designer bags such as luxury handbags, luxury tote bags, women's messenger bags and backpacks, women's luxury travel bags, as well as ultra-practical 2-in-1 belt pouches adapt ideally to daily needs and allow all the essentials to be carried. For evening wear or special occasions, modern luxury bags and luxury miniature bags in ultra-matte leather, patent leather or velvet brighten up outfits with their jewel-like style and metal chain. Each Dior women's luxury bag, handbag or purse combines the House's savoir-faire and ideals of elegance and modernity. All of the designer leather goods are meticulously crafted by hand, by the artisans of the Christian Dior ateliers, devoted to respecting the manufacturing processes with great care to achieving the highest standards of quality. Some of these iconic ladies designer bags can be personalized thanks to the House's range of embroidered shoulder straps.
